Delete your Sormi account

Sormi is developed by Eetu Koljonen.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Sormi and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. In Profile, choose Delete account, then confirm with Delete.
  3. If the Apple sign-in sheet appears, confirm using the Apple ID linked to your Sormi account.

Request deletion by email

Email support@sormi.app from the email address on your Sormi account and ask to delete it. If you used Apple’s Hide My Email, your account address is the Apple relay address. You can reply to a message forwarded through that relay from the mailbox linked to your Apple ID. If the addresses do not match, support will ask you to use the app or write from the account address.

The developer will complete your request within 30 days and reply when it is done. If you used Sign in with Apple and requested deletion by email, also remove Sormi in your Apple Account’s Sign in with Apple settings.

What gets deleted?

Deletion removes your account and all backed-up training, including routines, sessions, sets, bodyweight, soreness and notes. Copies can remain in daily database backups for up to seven days.

Training on your phone stays there. To remove it too, open Profile while signed out and choose Erase training on this phone.
