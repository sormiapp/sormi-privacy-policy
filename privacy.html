Sormi privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who runs Sormi

Sormi is made by an independent developer. For questions about this policy, write to sormiapp@gmail.com.

What is stored, and where

Sormi stores your routines, training sessions, bodyweight entries, notes and settings (such as Sound and Vibrate). All of it stays on your phone. The app does not send it anywhere.

What is not collected

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No tracking and no advertising.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting inside the app.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

If you install Sormi through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and any feedback you choose to send. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. The developer can see it in App Store Connect.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data is deleted with it. The developer has no copy, so it cannot be recovered.

Children

Sormi collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Accounts and backup may come to Sormi later. If they do, this policy will be updated before they ship.
