Sormi privacy policy

Updated 25 November 2026

Eetu Koljonen, Finland, develops Sormi and is the controller of your personal data. For privacy requests, email support@sormi.app.

Training and optional backup

You can train signed out. Your routines, sessions, sets, bodyweight, finger soreness and notes then stay on your phone. If you create an account, Sormi uses your email address and account ID to provide the backup and sync service you request. The legal basis is performance of that contract.

Before your first backup, Sormi asks you to tap Back up. This gives explicit consent to store health data, including bodyweight and soreness, with your training. Sormi records when you consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Services that handle data

  • Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany, handles sign-in and stores your backup. Resend in the EU delivers account confirmation and password reset emails. Their operational logs can include IP addresses and follow each provider’s log retention period.
  • If you choose Apple or Google sign-in where offered, that provider handles authentication. Sormi uses the sign-in result to identify your account.
  • In builds with crash reporting, Sentry in the EU receives error reports and technical details such as the stack trace and app version, whether you are signed in or out. Reports exclude your account ID and training. The legal basis is the developer’s legitimate interest in finding and fixing crashes. Reports expire after 30 days.
  • Expo EAS Update in the US handles app update checks, including an installation identifier.
  • If you install Sormi through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Some builds, such as early test builds, have no sign-in, crash reporting or update checks. In those, nothing leaves your phone.

Sormi does not sell your data, share it for advertising, track you across apps or use product analytics.

How long your backup lasts

Your backup stays until you delete your account, even if you stop using Sormi. Deletion removes the account and backed-up training from the live service. Copies can remain in daily database backups for up to seven days.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account in Profile or email from your account address. For the steps, see Delete your Sormi account. Email support@sormi.app to request access, export or correction, to request restricted processing or to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Age and policy changes

Sormi is for people aged 13 and over. You must be at least 13 to create an account. Changes to this policy will appear here with a new date.
