Sormi support

Email support@sormi.app for help with Sormi.

How do I move my training to a new phone?

On your old phone, open Profile, sign in and choose Back up. Connect to the internet and wait for sync to finish. On your new phone, sign in to the same account to download your training.

How do I sign in?

Open Profile and choose Continue with email or, on iPhone, Sign in with Apple. If your version offers Continue with Google, you can use that too. For a new email account, open the confirmation email on your phone.

What if I forgot my password?

In Profile, choose Continue with email, enter your account email address and tap Forgot password? Open the reset email on the phone where Sormi is installed and choose a new password.

How do I delete my account?

In Profile, choose Delete account. If you cannot use the app, follow the account deletion instructions.

How do I erase training from this phone?

Sign out if needed, then open Profile and choose Erase training on this phone. This clears the local copy. Any training already backed up stays in your account.
